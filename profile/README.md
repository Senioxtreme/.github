# Open Source is ♥

**SenioXtreme** is a registered **nonprofit association based in Palazzuolo sul Senio, Italy**, promoting outdoor sports, environmental awareness, and digital innovation for our local community.

We build **open and community-driven digital tools** that support local events, weather monitoring, and civic engagement — all powered by open-source software.

---

## 💡 Our Mission

To combine **technology and community spirit**.  
We believe digital innovation can strengthen small local communities and make information more open, useful, and accessible to everyone.

---

## 🤝 Community & Collaboration

We’re a **volunteer-driven** initiative — developers, photographers, athletes, and locals working together.  
We welcome collaborations with other nonprofits, schools, or developers who want to contribute to community-tech projects.

If you’re interested, feel free to open an issue or reach out to us via [senioxtreme.it](https://senioxtreme.it).
